## Remaining gaps from this audit

These are residual transparency/accessibility gaps, not newly discovered
numerical contradictions or reasons to reverse the panel's exploratory PASS.

1. **Realized development utilization remains less visible than training reuse.**
   The paper correctly labels Figure 3 as eligible support and states that
   development sampling is uniform with replacement, can leave IDs unused and
   reuses one recipe across observed seeds. It does not export the realized
   number of distinct development IDs, per-ID use range or repeated whole
   development tuples corresponding to the training realization display. A
   future metadata-only supplement could quantify that dependence without new
   fitting. Eligibility counts must not be relabeled as realized use.
2. **Physical task orientation is thin for a reader outside this workspace.**
   The measurement streams and operational marker semantics are clear, but the
   physical cue/navigation interface is mostly delegated to references. Any
   addition should distinguish documented task facts from unknown acquisition
   protocol details rather than inventing a richer task description.
3. **Accessibility and print reading remain incomplete.** The delivered PDF is
   untagged, and the historical Figure 12 axes require zoom despite its readable
   external legend and caption. Grayscale/print rendering was not tested. A
   tagged or HTML alternative and larger historical panels would help if this
   becomes an accessibility-reviewed publication artifact.
4. **Binary reproducibility needs an environment boundary.** The original
   ledger records its software versions, but the PR's shorter deterministic
   wording could be read as promising cross-environment byte identity. The
   follow-up found identical numeric tables and effectively identical pixels,
   not identical binaries under different library versions.

The strongest scientific limitations were already disclosed rather than newly
found: known-label construction is not unknown-trial prediction; exposed
development prevents pristine confirmation; dependent sources and repeated
blocks preclude row-IID or biological intervals; five diagnostic worlds are not
calibrated significance tests; missing subject/anatomical/nuisance/spatial
metadata prevent broader biological attribution; and ordered-path, frozen
cross-phase transfer and reusable-state observability claims remain untested.
