Broader training helped CEBRA-Time.
What, exactly, got better?

One teach unit: review the completed cue + navigation → cue-only transfer experiment, and distinguish a better representation pipeline from evidence of prospective path coding.

1 · What I understand

The scientific question is whether activity during the cue period contains information about the subsequently observed path beyond start and goal. CEBRA is one representation-learning instrument for that question—not the hypothesis and not the decoder.

Fact · narrow result

Giving the Time encoder training-source navigation activity improved the same cue-only logistic readout: mean balanced accuracy 32.6% → 61.8%, and probability loss 1.010 → 0.711. The broader model still missed the declared success rule and had unstable absolute discrimination.

High confidence

The executed comparison, saved predictions, metric calculations, native-pair construction and cue-only prediction boundary are documented and checked.

Still unknown

Which aspect of broader training caused the gain, whether it will recur in genuinely new sources, and whether any association reflects path information rather than unresolved sensory/history/selection effects.

Keep the levels separate: sorted-unit spike timestamps are recorded data; bins and cross-session rows are derived measurements; CEBRA coordinates are learned features; a biological planning interpretation is a much stronger inference.

“Held out” here means source-disjoint historical validation within existing sessions—not new sessions, new animals, or an untouched confirmatory test. The source pool was exposed in earlier research.

2 · The whole pipeline—and the boundary that matters

On narrow screens, scroll diagrams and tables sideways; the captions and text remain readable at normal size.

The analogy to NLP is a frozen pretrained encoder plus a linear probe. But here both encoders start from matched initialization: one learns from cue samples only, the other from a mixture. This is not navigation pretraining followed by cue fine-tuning, and the cue encoder is not fed an entire future trial.

Naming trap

The code calls the broader arm full_time. “Full” does not mean every millisecond: it retains the original first-second cue window plus all complete native 100-ms navigation bins. The small cue tail beyond one second and final partial navigation bins are excluded and audited.

4 · How it works, end to end

4.1 · What is one “population observation” here?

A pseudopopulation is an assembled vector whose units come from separate recording sessions. For each synthetic row, choose a real source trial from each session within the same known path class. Preserve all recorded units and cue bins from that source block. Join the blocks to make 29 features per bin.

There are 64 training rows per class. Validation enumerates 30 class-conditioned combinations—24 absent and six present—reusing 22 real sources. These are not 30 independent biological trials. Equal class mass in evaluation also means the 24:6 combination ratio is not an estimated natural path prevalence.

The most important qualification to “unsupervised Time”

Time's positive-pair rule does not directly optimize the path label. But the input assembly already depends on known labels, including validation assembly. This is a legitimate conditional source-pool benchmark, not a deployable unknown-label natural-trial predictor. Label-free objective ≠ label-free pipeline.

4.2 · What Time learns—and what it cannot see

Let x be one standardized 29-feature bin and z=fθ(x) its eight-coordinate embedding. The cosine contrastive objective rewards similarity to a designated nearby-time positive relative to sampled background observations. Background negatives are not guaranteed to have a different path label.

One encoder input: x(t) → z(t)
Training relationship: make z(t) relatively similar to z(t + 100 ms)
Prediction input: ten cue embeddings, never navigation embeddings

Time is a rule for choosing training pairs, not a recurrent architecture. The offset1 encoder processes a single bin. The classifier sees all ten cue bins because their embeddings are concatenated in order, not because CEBRA has learned a sequence-to-sequence decoder.

Why variable-duration navigation needs special care

Source trials have different navigation lengths. The code does not interpolate spike counts, pad missing activity with zeros, or assume equal physical positions at equal fractional progress. It builds a pair bank—a lookup table of valid anchor/next-bin pairs.

For one pseudorow, let L=max(n_bins−1) across its session blocks. For pair-bank row j, choose q=(j+.5)/L; a source with n bins supplies k=floor(q×(n−1)) and k+1.

Every within-pair step is a genuine next native bin in its own source. But consecutive pair-bank rows may repeat one source while advancing another. Chaining them as a real time series would be wrong. The coverage audit checks that every native adjacent pair is represented; it does not prove physical-position alignment or a simultaneous population trajectory.

Sampling chooses phase, then pseudorow uniformly, then a valid pair/bin. Longer navigation does not receive extra pseudorow probability simply because its bank has more entries; repeated entries within a bank remain part of the declared sampling design.

Balanced accuracy averages the two class recalls, so always predicting absent gives 50%, not 80%. AUROC measures whether a present row tends to receive a higher score than an absent row; 0.5 is uninformative ranking. The cue-trained model's very low AUROC indicates reversed ranking on this pool—not a reason to flip labels after inspecting validation.

The mean recall shift reveals a concrete change: the cue-trained logistic model missed every present combination at its fixed threshold in all three seeds. The broader version recovered some of those positives, very unevenly across seeds. It did not solve every type of error.

4.4 · Why 61.8% accuracy can coexist with worse-than-baseline loss

Thresholded classification ignores probability confidence. Log loss evaluates the probability assigned to the true outcome: −log(p_true). A correct but tentative prediction and an incorrect, highly confident prediction are not symmetric.

Correct but tentative

p_true=.60 → loss ≈ 0.511

Better than a 50/50 guess by about 0.182 nats.

Incorrect and confident

p_true=.10 → loss ≈ 2.303

Worse than a 50/50 guess by about 1.610 nats.

Illustrative probabilities, not measurements from this experiment. They show why several modestly correct decisions need not compensate for a confident error. Above-baseline loss is not, by itself, a pure calibration diagnosis; weak or reversed ranking also matters here.

Do not report the 0.300-nat gain as “0.300 nats of biological information.” It is a difference between two model risks, not an estimate of conditional mutual information.

Broader training fits the training assemblies slightly less well while transferring better to the historical validation pool. The RBF-SVM—a nonlinear classifier with fixed C=1, gamma=scale—also shows relative Time improvement, but its thresholded BA stays around chance. Its margins are not probabilities; we did not fit a probability-calibration model or report SVM log loss.

Behavior is not helped on the primary probability-loss metric. Its accuracy increases slightly, so “broader training harms every Behavior metric” would also be incorrect. Behavior uses same-class positives across the phase mixture; Time uses within-phase native adjacency. They are different training constraints. Why they respond differently is untested.

The raw/PCA controls use this experiment's exact cue assemblies and readouts. Do not import the PCA performance from an earlier frozen-embedding/readout study: cohort overlap does not make differently assembled or trained benchmarks numerically interchangeable.

4.8 · Relative null, absolute null, and source sensitivity

The 20 null worlds shuffle source labels within session × split before rebuilding the pipeline, preserving a coherent label across each source's cue and navigation. Each world repeats all three seeds. The inclusive plus-one tail is (1 + number of null statistics ≥ observed)/(20 + 1); its finest possible value is 1/21≈.0476.

  • Time paired gain: 1/21. No shuffled world had an equally large Time gain.
  • Maximum across Time and Behavior gains: 3/21. This addresses the declared two-objective family, not the entire project's adaptive history.
  • Absolute broader-Time loss: 12/21. Relative improvement is not equivalent to strong absolute decoding.

These are limited-resolution no-association references, not general equal-risk tests between algorithms. Serial exchangeability—whether label shuffling appropriately represents the relevant null given trial dependence—remains unresolved. Historical validation exposure also remains.

5 · Review the design, not just the score

Three explanations the experiment does not separate

Broader activity coverage

Navigation exposes the encoder to activity patterns absent from the narrow cue training distribution.

Useful temporal relationships

Native navigation adjacency may supply better contrastive structure for transferable features.

Less cue repetition

A fixed 50/50 budget reduces cue exposure, potentially reducing specialization to a small cue source pool.

Inference, not observed mechanism
All three are compatible with the result. The intervention changes input coverage, positive/negative sampling distributions and cue exposure together. Calling the gain proof that navigation “taught planning” chooses a mechanism the design did not identify.

Bad design if used for the wrong claim

Treating the 30 label-conditioned combinations as 30 independent, simultaneous, unknown-label trials would be bad design because both the independence and measurement assumptions are false. Consequence: inflated evidence and an impossible deployment story. Smallest fix: retain source-aware conditional inference and call it that. If the claim must concern a natural same-trial population state, obtain the recording/identity evidence that supports it. Do not pretend more bootstraps repair missing simultaneity.

The existing conditional benchmark itself is not thereby invalid. It remains a useful way to ask whether condition-associated responses transfer across disjoint source pools. Individual-cell significance is not a gate, and a simultaneous 195-unit recording is not a prerequisite for all condition-level progress.

Judgment · an intentional tradeoff

The 1,000-update comparison is a small, auditable engineering experiment. It is adequate for this frozen intervention, but underidentifies why it works and is not a verdict on CEBRA's optimal performance. Do not expand tuning after seeing the result and continue to call the same validation set confirmatory.

Review next—not a queued experiment

  1. Mechanism control: preserve navigation observations, phase weights and computational budget while disrupting positive-pair temporal adjacency under a newly reviewed protocol. Persistence of the gain would weaken the claim that the particular adjacency structure is necessary; it would not prove which alternative caused it.
  2. Cue-exposure control: compare against a separately specified cue-only exposure match. Matching cue exposure and matching total optimization are different controls; do not claim that one automatically matches both.
  3. Scientific follow-up: genuinely independent sources and verified task/recording metadata, with endpoint and available cue/history/view controls. Keep all derivatives of a source together.

No such follow-up was run for this lesson. No model, checkpoint, threshold, target, data split or success criterion was changed.

What has actually been verified?

The original run completed 252 encoders, 63 PCA transforms and 756 readouts. Its 1,071 fitted artifacts were replayed without refitting; 4,681 files were hash-checked. Independent numerical QA recomputed 1,512 metric groups from 119,448 saved predictions. The reported original software suite passed 113 tests. Those are historical execution receipts, not new tests or new biological fits performed to write this lesson.

AI-assisted independent reviews are software/scientific QA, not independent laboratory replication or peer review. Parent personally read the load-bearing protocol, sampler, assembly, decoder, summary and sensitivity code; a fresh read-only scout checked the source map.

7 · Teach-back: make a review decision

A colleague writes: “61.8% accuracy and a 1/21 null tail show that pretraining uncovered prospective path coding.” Would you approve that sentence? Rewrite it using one relative-improvement fact and one absolute-performance or measurement limitation.

Open only after you have drafted your answer

A defensible rewrite: “Under the frozen conditional-pseudopopulation benchmark, broader Time training improved cue-only logistic loss in all three seeds and mean balanced accuracy rose to 61.8%. However, mean loss remained worse than the constant-probability reference, absolute null support was weak, and discrimination varied strongly by seed. This is an exploratory representation-transfer result, not established prospective path coding.”

The decisive move is to keep relative model improvement, absolute prediction, and biological interpretation separate. Mentioning the known-label-conditioned measurement object is an additional strong review point.
