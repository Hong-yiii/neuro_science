Executive summary

This week clarified both the methods and the limits of the biological inference: endpoint-condition separability is strong in the constructed benchmark, but reliable cue-period path information beyond endpoints has not been established.

The objective is H1a: incremental prediction of the subsequently observed path beyond start and goal; H1b: reproducible low-dimensional organization of that information. The latest study tested a narrower binary interior-component target, not full-path reconstruction, intention or causal planning. H1b and the later artificial-agent comparison (H2) were not tested.

Main advances: diagnosed CEBRA sampler/readout sensitivity on the easier endpoint task; localized path transfer failure to source-pool generalization rather than only one synthetic assembly; completed a complementary per-unit real-trial analysis and a bounded preprocessing study; preserved and verified the full local research record in one workspace.

Interpretation: stronger regularization improved probability loss within the latest tested family, but its historical diagnostic remained weak and heterogeneous. The results neither establish H1 nor demonstrate that H1 is false or would necessarily succeed with more data.

Recommended discussion: retain the simple raw-rate/variance-floor/strong-L2 baseline; verify task/history/recording support; decide a worthwhile effect and sensitivity target; then define a prospectively frozen follow-up on genuinely new source sets. No new experiment is initiated by this report.

02 · Evaluation contract

Study design and the scope of the claim

The active archive contains 195 place-selected unit spike trains across 58 sessions. This is not a verified simultaneous 195-neuron population. Different tasks use different supported subsets; archive size must not substitute for the effective source count of a particular comparison.

The population analyses use condition-level, label-conditioned pseudopopulations: original source trials are split first, then same-condition session-specific cell×time blocks are assembled within each split. Evaluation construction uses the true condition label. This tests reproducibility of conditional response differences across source pools, not operational unknown-label decoding of a natural simultaneous trial. Cross-session covariance and exact trial-specific population dynamics are not recovered. A separate per-unit real-trial analysis complements, rather than invalidates, this lane.

These cohorts/targets/metrics differ; their scores are not a single accuracy leaderboard or a cross-study preprocessing ablation. Cells, bins, exact products and seeds do not create independent biological trials. Source separation also does not by itself remove serial dependence or changing session state.

Validation status: the original holdout was exposed during earlier development. New CV outcome separation is useful, but historical validation and panel eligibility remain development-conditioned; resplitting or repository cleanup does not restore pristine confirmation. [S1, S5, S8, S10]

03 · Completed endpoint work

Endpoint decoding: a useful methods result

Initial joint development accuracy was 82.50% PCA8/bin, 42.78% CEBRA-Time and 18.61% CEBRA-Behavior, averaged over three seeds. A stronger frozen-feature logistic readout (C=1→0.01) improved these to 83.61%, 51.67% and 25.56%, respectively. This helped, but did not alone close CEBRA’s initial gap. [A1, S3, S10]

At temperature 0.3 and 250 updates, matching Behavior positives by endpoint and cue bin improved accuracy 53.06→83.33% with global negatives. Extending that same setting to 3,000 updates reduced accuracy to 64.86%. More training was not automatically better. Additional time matching of negatives also did not generally help.

Increasing synthetic training rows/class from 16 to 64 improved one time-matched setting 59.58→81.53%, with matched PCA 83.61→84.86%. That changed sampling/coverage of existing sources, not the biological sample size. The separate stronger-weight-decay cross-entropy recipe collapsed to chance; “regularize everything more” is not a supported general conclusion.

Takeaway: sampler semantics, readout penalty and finite-source generalization matter. Some CEBRA settings became descriptively competitive with PCA. No independently validated CEBRA advantage, prospective path effect or low-dimensional path organization follows from endpoint accuracy. [S3, S10]

04 · Completed path work

Path generalization remained non-supportive

Earlier V6–V9 and Tracks A/B

The early whole-cue binary path benchmark gave BA 45.3% raw totals, 47.4% raw ten-bin, 52.1% PCA3 (unstable), 45.3% PCA8, 44.3% Time8, and 41.4% Behavior8, versus 50% constant. PCA3’s three-seed range was 25.8–78.9%. A perfect selected 0.2–0.3 s bin did not survive the max-over-ten-bin diagnostic null (p≈0.218). [S2, S10]

Track A enumerated 24 absent + 6 present empirical validation combinations, with each class receiving half the weight. This removed assembly-sampling noise, not uncertainty in the small original pools. More synthetic rows brought ridge near exact BA≈0.479 rather than rescuing transfer.

The primary CEBRA grid fitted training at BA=1.0 and generalized to other assemblies of the same training sources at 0.9925 Time / 0.9977 Behavior. On source-disjoint exact validation, means were 0.2492 / 0.3517. The failure therefore extends beyond memorizing a particular assembled training matrix. Its cause—drift, finite-source variation, measurement, weak accessible signal or confounds—remains unidentified.

The fixed 14-configuration Track B family’s best observed BA was 7/12≈58.33%, with corrected family p 19/21≈0.905. This exact inclusive-tie result supersedes the older raw 0.857 value; models and predictions did not change. [S4, A3]

05 · Bounded preprocessing study

Latest experiment: what preprocessing could—and could not—fix

The September 8 study held fixed the target D_N_C_M__N_C_B, central-middle→central-bottom occurrence among all interior corridor runs after dropping first and last. It is a binary component of the later ordered path, not whole-path reconstruction or “edge anywhere.” Neural inputs cover exactly the first [0,1) seconds of the operational cue. Totals: 147 train + 41 historical-validation sources; 49 unique unit IDs; 13 sessions. Unit/session counts overlap across panels. The two smallest panels are point/influence-only; many larger-panel validation strata are also sparse. Eligibility used behavior, QC, duration and existing validation class support, not neural success. This is openly development-conditioned selection, not confirmatory cohort discovery. [S5–S6]

Protocol: 18 initial recipes crossed 1/2/5 temporal bins × raw/square-root/log1p transforms × standard/variance-floor scaling at L2 λ=0.1. Three inner source-held-out folds selected the representation; only that winner received the predeclared λ=0.01 and 1 refinements. The final winner among 20 was first-second raw rates, training-derived variance-floor scaling, λ=1 logistic regression. No CEBRA/PCA, new target or posthoc label inversion was added.

Primary score: Δlogloss = log(2) − class-balanced logloss, in nats, averaged equally across endpoint panels. Positive beats constant p=0.5 under this constructed distribution. BA is secondary. The average-loss penalty is implemented as C=1/(λNtraining rows), so repeated synthetic rows cannot silently weaken regularization.

Stronger λ improved probability loss even while CV accuracy declined. Five-bin models could fit training better and generalize worse; this is compatible with finite-source estimation limits, not proof against temporal neural coding. Variance flooring is a feature-weighting prior, not a validated biological noise model. Probability-loss improvement is not a separately demonstrated calibration result.

The selected historical result was Δ=+0.02858; BA=55%, versus CV Δ=+0.09261. Only 2/5 endpoint deltas were positive. Removing 6→1 changed the remaining mean to −0.03787. The 6→1 panel’s 100% BA pertains to small known-label combinations, not perfect classification of 17 unknown natural simultaneous trials.

For the same raw/floor representation, λ=1 reduced historical logloss from 0.8473 to 0.6646 relative to λ=0.1. This is a within-study numerical improvement, not broad validated path decoding and not a matched ablation against earlier studies’ different panels. The endpoint-dominated result is a follow-up lead, not a replacement for the frozen five-panel estimand. [A5–A7, S6]

06 · Robustness and validity

Controls, uncertainty and remaining confounds

Each of 100 ordinary and 100 temporal-half source-label permutation worlds repeated its own 18-arm CV search and its own L2 refinement. Both the null-CV-selected and null-max-family references were compared with the same observed CV-selected statistic. The max-family reference is deliberately conservative; all policies are reported rather than choosing the favorable p.

P-values use inclusive ≥ ties and the plus-one correction. They are assumption-dependent null comparisons, not the probability that H1 is false. None supplied convincing separation from its declared null. [A6]

  • Source sensitivity: 200 fixed-recipe source bootstraps gave empirical 2.5/50/97.5 percentiles −0.08751 / +0.01538 / +0.10061, spanning zero. These are sensitivity quantiles, not selection-adjusted or animal-level confidence intervals.
  • Sparse validation: 17/28 session×endpoint×class pools are singletons. Resampling them cannot reveal variation in additional unobserved trials.
  • Temporal-null limitations: 52/101 strata were pure-label, containing 64/188 sources whose labels cannot change under that rule. Median individual source-label changes were 44.7% ordinary and 31.4% temporal-half. Temporal stratification is not proven serial exchangeability.
  • Assembly sensitivity: fixed-recipe seeds 1/2 gave Δ +0.01808 / +0.02071, not independent biological replications.
  • Influence: 12/13 session-removal refit aggregates stayed positive, but overlapping refits ranged −0.00393 to +0.07417. Common-panel comparisons are needed when removing a session drops an endpoint. This does not overcome the clear 6→1 dominance.
  • Nuisance-only control: fixed-λ trial index/full-cue duration had historical Δ −0.35606. It was neither optimized nor a nested nuisance+neural conditional test; it does not exclude nonlinear time/history/gaze/heading/state confounds.

Under the reported rule, previous goal equals current start; fixing the current endpoint pair also fixes previous-goal identity. Other history variables can still vary. Authoritative task/marker/recording-group semantics and cue/history covariates remain important unresolved checks. The place-selected cohort and previous development exposure constrain generality. No power/sensitivity analysis yet justifies an absence claim. [S1, S6–S7, A8]

Methodological reading, not evidence for this dataset

  • Schneider, Lee & Mathis (2023), Nature — CEBRA. 10.1038/s41586-023-06031-6, plus official usage documentation. Auxiliary variables and sampling rules shape the learned embedding; our result still needs separate source-held-out evaluation.
  • Kriegeskorte et al. (2009), Nature Neuroscience — circular analysis. 10.1038/nn.2303. Selection and evaluation need appropriate independence under the null; the paper does not prescribe this dataset’s particular split.
  • Varoquaux (2018), NeuroImage — small-sample cross-validation uncertainty. 10.1016/j.neuroimage.2017.06.061. More correlated rows are not more independent evidence; no universal minimum sample count is inferred here.
  • Pillow et al. (2008), Nature — population correlations. 10.1038/nature07140. Correlations can matter for population information. This is a general warning about missing simultaneity, not a hippocampal result replicated by our assemblies.
  • scikit-learn 1.7 API/source. LogisticRegression, log loss, balanced accuracy. Smaller C means stronger regularization; probability loss and average class recall answer different questions. Historical runtime: sklearn 1.7.2; the project’s C=1/(λN) mapping is additionally explicit in its source.

The bounded read-only methods review checked source/abstract or official API scope. These citations motivate controls, not the superiority of a bin width, this variance floor, or a biological mechanism.
