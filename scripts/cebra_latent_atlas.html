How to read the numbers

  • 3D retained energy measures how much the displayed axes retain, relative to the training center. It is not prediction accuracy. Whole-cue and cue-bin spaces are different objects.
  • Participation ratio summarizes the full-space linear variance spectrum. It is not intrinsic or biological dimensionality. A curved one-dimensional loop can occupy many axes.
  • Distance correlation / neighbor overlap compare full-space distances to the displayed 3D distances on a fixed bounded sample. Same-row comparisons are excluded; different rows still reuse sources. No p-values or confidence intervals are implied. Ties can make k-neighbor overlap unstable.
  • Condition-RDM agreement compares full-space distances between matched condition means. For cue-bin views, only within-bin condition distances are combined. No result is reported for V9's two conditions. CEBRA-Behavior is trained to structure its condition geometry.
  • CEBRA-Time preserves valid within-pseudorow bin adjacency, but label-conditioned assembly means the full pipeline is not label-free. Time τ=1 versus selected Behavior τ=.3 is not a pure objective-only comparison.
  • Cosine-trained CEBRA normalizes each bin embedding onto a hypersphere. A spherical shell may reflect that constraint. We did not run a topology test.
